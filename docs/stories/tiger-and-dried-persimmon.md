# The Tiger and the Dried Persimmon — production note

## Source note

`호랑이와 곶감` is a nationally distributed Korean animal folktale, also known in variants as “A Dried Persimmon More Frightening than a Tiger” and “The Thief Who Caught a Tiger.” The production follows the compact account documented by the [Encyclopedia of Korean Culture](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0063735): a tiger hears a mother fail to quiet her child with the threat of a tiger, sees the child stop for a dried persimmon, mistakes `gotgam` for a fearsome being, and later mistakes a cattle thief on its back for that same being. At dawn the thief recognizes the tiger and jumps away. Later bear, hollow-tree, and revenge episodes are established variants, but are omitted here so the film ends on the mutual mistake.

No dynasty, named village, named historical person, or specific historical costume is assigned. The setting is only an unnamed Korean mountain village on a cold night. `Gotgam` is shown as a peeled, dried persimmon, consistent with the [Encyclopedia of Korean Culture’s food entry](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0004560). The contradiction is spatial and comic: one small fruit remains inside the house while fear enlarges it into a monster in the tiger’s mind; in the dark, tiger and thief each mistake the other for something safer or more dangerous than it is.

## Visual grammar

- Palette: pine ink `#07141d`, moon indigo `#18334a`, hanji `#eee2c6`, persimmon `#e4612d`, leaf green `#3f654f`, soot `#151719`.
- Materials: low-poly matte surfaces with shallow relief, like cut and folded mulberry paper; no raster textures.
- Composition: side-on folk-painting silhouettes interrupted by close, low camera poses that put the viewer inside each mistaken belief.
- Signature transition: a single dried persimmon multiplies into a moving tunnel of instanced orange forms around the tiger, then collapses back to the untouched fruit at dawn.
- Instancing: hanging persimmons, fear-field persimmons, pine trunks/crowns, roof tiles, and tiger stripes share geometry and materials through `THREE.InstancedMesh`.
- Default playback: Indonesian voice with Chinese subtitles.

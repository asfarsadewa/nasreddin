# Anansi and the Pot — production note

## Source note

This production follows the Akan/Twi tale recorded by R. S. Rattray in *Akan-Ashanti Folk-Tales* (1930) as “How It Came About That Wisdom Came Among the Tribe.” A [Twi transcription and Rattray's English translation](https://www.theakan.com/akan-folktales-as-transmitters-of-indigenous-wisdom/) preserve the compact action: Kwaku Ananse gathers all wisdom into a gourd pot, ties it in front of himself, and repeatedly fails to climb a tree. His small son Ntikuma suggests carrying the gourd on his back. Ananse succeeds, realizes that the child possessed wisdom he had missed, and tears the gourd loose so its contents scatter.

Later retellings commonly call the tale “Anansi and the Pot of Wisdom” and may add Nyame giving Anansi the pot, a named silk-cotton tree, rain spreading the wisdom, or a concluding speech about sharing. Those additions are omitted. No period, named settlement, costume, or specific tree species is assigned. The story retains the popular spelling `Anansi` in its English title while using the Akan form `Kwaku Ananse` in narration. The pot is shown as a hollowed gourd rather than fired clay, following the recorded text.

The contradiction is physical: the character who believes he has contained every scrap of wisdom cannot see the simple solution visible to the small child below him. The staging lets that contradiction resolve through action rather than an added moral.

## Twelve-beat treatment

| Beat | Chapter | Speaker | Visual and camera purpose | Sound event |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wisdom gathered | Narrator | A twilight clearing appears as luminous fragments stream toward Ananse. | Opening seperewa-like motif; forest dusk ambience. |
| 2 | Wisdom gathered | Narrator | Macro orbit around a gourd filling with restless golden motes. | Dry seeds, wood and glass-like wisdom shimmer. |
| 3 | Wisdom gathered | Kwaku Ananse | Low, proud portrait; the gourd closes and the surrounding world darkens. | Score narrows and drops beneath his claim. |
| 4 | The tree | Narrator | A vertical reveal makes the tree tower over Ananse and the front-bound pot. | Rope draw and bark detail. |
| 5 | The tree | Narrator | Side profile: gourd strikes trunk and catches his legs. | Hollow gourd knocks against wood. |
| 6 | The tree | Narrator | Three failed climbs compress into one rising-and-sliding camera move. | Scraping bark, rope strain, quick drum breaths. |
| 7 | A small voice | Ntikuma | Camera drops beneath Ananse to the small spider pointing toward his back. | Music clears to one plucked reply. |
| 8 | A small voice | Kwaku Ananse | Tight high angle catches Ananse dismissing the child, still trapped by the pot. | One dry wooden rebuke. |
| 9 | The turn | Narrator | After another fall, the gourd travels in an arc from chest to back. | Rope shifts; the narrowed score begins to open. |
| 10 | The turn | Narrator | Camera cranes with Ananse as he climbs swiftly into the branches. | Fast bark rhythm and the recorded `kra, kra, kra`. |
| 11 | What wisdom missed | Kwaku Ananse | In the canopy, Ananse looks down at tiny Ntikuma; the gourd pulses behind him. | Underscore suspends around the realization. |
| 12 | What wisdom missed | Narrator | The gourd tears, falls, and bursts; instanced wisdom becomes a glowing web across the clearing. | Calabash split, `tintini`, `tesee`, and the open ending cadence. |

## Visual grammar

- Palette: deep indigo `#07131b`, laterite `#8f3f28`, gourd gold `#d89a43`, leaf green `#1e493d`, raffia `#d8c38e`, wisdom turquoise `#51b7a6`.
- Materials: matte carved-wood silhouettes, fibrous rope, smooth hollow gourd, and jewel-like wisdom fragments; no raster imagery.
- Composition: the tree is a vertical measuring line. Ananse repeatedly occupies the cramped strip between trunk and pot while Ntikuma remains small but spatially clear below.
- Signature transition: wisdom begins as dozens of separate fragments squeezed into one gourd. When the gourd breaks, those same instances race outward and settle into a vast luminous web linking roots, branches, and horizon.
- Instancing: forest leaves, distant trunks, ground seeds, and wisdom fragments share geometry and materials through `THREE.InstancedMesh`.
- Default playback: English voice with Indonesian subtitles.
